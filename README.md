**SportTracker** was built with a clear goal: to provide a powerful, open-source alternative to mainstream fitness tracking apps like Strava or Google Health. Whether you are looking to track your daily steps, visualize your athletic progression, or get AI-generated workout routines, SportTracker is designed to give you control over your health data.

*This project is fully open-source and licensed under Apache 2.0.*

* 🏃 **Live Running Tracking:** Hit the road and track your runs in real-time. The app records your GPS route, calculates your average speed, and tracks your distance.

* 📊 **Data Visualization:** True to my Data Analysis background, you can visualize your evolution and performance trends for any specific exercise.
* 👣 **Built-in Pedometer:** Seamlessly count your daily steps.

## 💻 Technology Stack

SportTracker relies on a modern and efficient tech stack to ensure smooth performance and reliable data handling:

* **Database & Backend:** Powered by **Firebase**. This ensures secure data storage, fast real-time syncing, and reliable backend infrastructure for all your fitness data.
* **Artificial Intelligence:** The AI workout generation is driven by **OpenRouter**, allowing the app to leverage powerful, state-of-the-art Large Language Models (LLMs) to create smart and personalized training plans.
* **Mobile Development:** Designed to efficiently handle device hardware like GPS for run tracking and internal sensors for step counting.

## 🚀 Roadmap (Upcoming Features)

I am constantly working on improving SportTracker. Here is what you can expect in future updates:

* ⭐ **Program Rating System:** A feature to rate and review the effectiveness of your weekly and bi-weekly generated programs.
